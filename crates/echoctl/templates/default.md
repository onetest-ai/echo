## Summary
One sentence: what the meeting was for. Then **Highlights**: 3–7 bullets, each `**Topic:** what was said, with names
and numbers [mm:ss]`. Then **Risks & issues** as bullets, only when some were raised. Then **Key dates**: up to three
dates or deadlines that were said, each `**date** — what falls on it [mm:ss]`, only when any were.

## Decisions
Each decision in one line, with who made or owns it when clear.

## Action items
`- [ ] owner — task (due, if stated)`. "Me" is the user. Only commitments someone actually made.

## Open questions
Unresolved points, disagreements, and things waiting on someone.
