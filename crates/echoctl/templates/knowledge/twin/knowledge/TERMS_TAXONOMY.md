## Usage Rules

1. Normalise an obvious transcript mistake to the canonical term when the context is clear.
2. Don't force a correction when the source clearly means something else.
3. Use the canonical spelling in titles, summaries and cleaned text.
4. When a new term keeps coming back and matters, add it here instead of fixing it page by page.
