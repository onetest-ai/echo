## Summary
One sentence: the overall state of the work. Then **Highlights**: 3–7 bullets, each `**Topic:** what was said, with
names and numbers [mm:ss]`. Then **Key dates**: up to three dates or deadlines that were said, each `**date** — what
falls on it [mm:ss]`, only when any were. Risks go under Blockers & risks.

## Progress
Per person or workstream: done since last time, doing next.

## Blockers & risks
Each blocker with who can unblock it.

## Action items
`- [ ] owner — task (due, if stated)`. "Me" is the user.
