# TC-001: The transcript has the right words, speakers and order

**Module:** Transcription | **Priority:** Critical | **Type:** Functional

## Preconditions
- Accessibility granted (for remote names)

## Steps

| # | Action | Expected Result |
|---|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Record a 3-minute call where each side reads a known short paragraph in turns | — |
| 2 | Open it → Transcript | Your lines are Me; the other side is named or Remote |
| 3 | Compare with the paragraphs | Recognisable, names and numbers mostly right, in time order; a quick reply after the line it answers |
| 4 | Look for invented filler | None ("Thanks for watching", "Subtitles by…", "[music]") |

## Expected Final State
An accurate, ordered transcript with the right speakers and no hallucinated filler.
